# Pitch format and plan (confirmed by Shyan, 15 Nov)

**Format:** 4 minutes. **2 minutes talking** (business case, how the app works, what it is for), then **2 minutes of demo, filmed in advance**. Deadline: everything finished Mon 16 Nov 13:00.

## Part 1: 2 minutes talking (about 280 words)

| Time | Beat | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:25 | Problem | Rehab stops when people leave hospital. They are sent home with a sheet of exercises and a few physio sessions. [One checked number here: e.g. share of stroke survivors with a weak arm, or therapy minutes received vs recommended. Source required.] |
| 0:25-0:45 | Why robots have not fixed it | Rehab robots exist. The largest trial (RATULS, 770 patients) found them no better than usual care, and each one is an expensive single-purpose machine. They tie on results and lose on cost. |
| 0:45-1:10 | Vita | Software that turns a standard robot arm into a rehab station. One dial: it helps a weak arm move, and challenges a strong one. It measures every repetition. |
| 1:10-1:35 | How it works for the patient | Your specialist sets your plan. You sit at any Vita station (clinic, gym, care home), scan it with the app, and the arm runs your exercises. Your reps, reach and force go to your physio in a weekly report. |
| 1:35-1:55 | Business | Stations are leased to locations; hardware is a commodity arm, the product is the software and the data. [Price and payback only if checked.] Next step: a pilot with [named clinic or location] to prove one therapist can supervise several stations. |
| 1:55-2:00 | Hand-over | "Here it is working." |

Rules: no number without a checked source (business/claims). Do not claim better recovery; the claim is the same therapy at lower cost, as a hypothesis. Say "research prototype".

## Part 2: 2-minute film (shot list)

| Time | Shot | What is on screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:10 | Wide: patient sits at the station, phone in hand | Arm at start pose, big screen behind | "This is a Vita station." |
| 0:10-0:20 | Phone close-up: scan the station, plan appears, tap Start forward reach | App | "She scans the station. Her plan loads." |
| 0:20-0:45 | Reach, assisted: limp arm carried out and back, 2 reps | Arm + big screen (HELPING, dial at +1) | "Full help. The arm feels how hard she pushes and does the rest." |
| 0:45-1:05 | She tries harder, 2-3 reps; the dial falls by itself | Big screen dial moving; phone rep counter | "As she does more, it does less. Nobody touches a control." |
| 1:05-1:15 | She lets go mid-reach; the arm stops | Close on hand and flange | "Let go, and it stops." |
| 1:15-1:35 | Push (or row): same arm now resists, 3 reps | Big screen CHALLENGING; force bar | "Same arm, now a strength trainer." |
| 1:35-1:45 | Strength test: one push and hold | Phone: peak force | "It measures. Readings on this prototype are capped at 30 N for safety." |
| 1:45-2:00 | Physio screen: today's reps, charts, session note | Physio screen | "Her physio sees every rep. The history shown is simulated; today's session is real." |

Optional second motion if it works on the arm: shoulder circles (5 s insert after push).

## Filming rules
- Only after robot/SAFETY.md is signed and the handle is mounted with its tool set.
- Film each shot separately and cut together; several takes each. Keep one continuous take of reach → dial falling as proof it is real.
- Landscape, tripod or propped phone, big screen readable in frame, REAL ROBOT badge visible.
- Screen-record the phone app and the big screen as clean inserts.
- Simulated data is labelled on screen wherever it appears.
- Cast: patient actor, operator, e-stop holder (out of frame, hand on the button).

## Film crew (three people: Shyan, Shiv, Kian)
- **Shiv: patient actor.** Holds the handle, starts each exercise in the app on the phone.
- **Kian: e-stop holder.** Out of frame, hand on the button for every take, does nothing else. Calls "clear" before each take.
- **Shyan: operator and camera.** Runs the operator console (home, prepare, go, resume), then films. Wide shots from a fixed tripod or propped phone; hand-held only for close-ups, never standing inside the arm's reach.
- Nobody is spare, so screen recordings (phone app, big screen, physio screen) are captured separately, not during takes.

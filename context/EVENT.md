# Event info — Leukerbad Robotics Carnival Hack 2026 (from opening slides, Sat 14 Nov)

## Track 4: Elderly Care & Rehabilitation (examples — you can define your own problem)
| Idea | What | Suggested kit |
|------|------|---------------|
| Fetch and hand over | Pick up an object and hand it over safely | Flexiv Rizon 4, WUJI hand, RealSense |
| Rehab coach | Guide exercises, count reps, give feedback | VIGX exoskeleton, Zeroth mini humanoid, RealSense |
| Check-in companion | Chat, remind, call for help after a fall | Reachy Mini, AI Cat, webcam |

- **Arms:** Flexiv Rizon 4, Franka Emika, 2x RobCo, AgileX arm, reBot Arm B601, 2x OpenArm + leader, 13x SO-101
- **Hands:** WUJI hand, Leadtron hand, VIGX exoskeleton, Meta Quest 3
- **Humanoids:** UniX AI, Zeroth mini humanoid, PrimeBot, 2x Reachy Mini, AI Cat
- **Mobile:** 2x JoyandAI bases, ROS mobile platform, Waveshare RaspRover, Hover drone
- **Compute/sensing:** 5x RDK X5, Jetson Orin Nano, 2x Raspberry Pi 5, 3x Omnicor chips, 5x NormaCore, MetaCam Lite scanner, RealSense, 2x webcams

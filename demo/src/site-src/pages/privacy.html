Privacy, in plain words.

What this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email, organisation (optional), the role you pick and your message. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on this website.

Why

We use your details only to answer your message and, if you ask, to talk about the pilot or a station. We do not sell or share them for marketing.

Where it is stored

The form is handled by our website host, Netlify, which stores the messages for us. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads.

Security

We store everything you send us in secure storage and encrypt it: on the way to us, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and while it is stored. Only the Vita team can read it.

Medical information

Please do not send medical information through this website. Vita is in development and the website is not part of any treatment.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete what you sent us. Send your request through the contact form and we will handle it.

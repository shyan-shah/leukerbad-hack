What a pilot practice does

Little extra work. A lot of extra practice.

Set the plans

Your therapists choose the exercises and limits for each patient, the way they would on paper today.

Keep an eye on it

A trained staff member stays near the station. No extra specialist is needed during sessions.

Read the reports

Every week you get a short report per patient, and you tell us what works and what does not.

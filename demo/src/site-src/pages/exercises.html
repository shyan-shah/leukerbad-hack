One session

15 to 30 minutes. Several sets, not one.

A patient never does an exercise just once. Each exercise is done in sets: a block of repetitions, then a rest, then the next set. Three or four exercises fill one session.

  • Sets build recovery. Repeating a movement many times, spread over sets, is what helps the body relearn it and get stronger.
  • Rest is part of the plan. 45 seconds to 2 minutes between sets, so every repetition is done well, not tired and sloppy.
  • The specialist sets the dose. Sets, repetitions and rest per patient. The arm counts every repetition and tells them when to rest.

What Vita does not do

  • Train walking, balance or fine hand function
  • Treat pain or the injury itself
  • Work with unhealed fractures or fresh surgical repairs
  • Train patients with acute inflammation or severe pain
  • Train patients who cannot say stop

When in doubt, the patient's specialist decides.

Home/About Vita

More practice for every patient, with the therapist in charge.

Vita started from one simple observation: recovery needs hundreds of repetitions, and there are not enough hands to guide them.

Why

A typical stroke session has about 32 arm movements. Recovery needs hundreds. Waiting lists for rehab can run over a year.

How

A robot arm guides the exercises a specialist sets, at a gym, care home, pool or community centre close to home.

Our rule

The therapist sets every limit. The robot never decides the treatment, and one red button always stops it.

Who advises us

Built with rehabilitation experts.

K

Katja, rehabilitation specialist at Klimmendaal

More than ten years in rehabilitation. The exercises were approved by Katja. At her centre this work is done fully by hand today, with waiting lists of over a year.

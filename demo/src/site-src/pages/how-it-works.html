Why a station

More practice between visits, for less.

Vita does not replace the physiotherapist. It fills the days between visits, when most recovery could happen but little practice does.

Physiotherapy alone

  • $75–150 per session without insurance (US)
  • Practice mostly happens during visits, once or twice a week
  • Every extra hour of training needs a therapist

With a Vita station

  • A fraction of the cost per session, as part of a monthly subscription
  • Train between visits, several times a week, so progress comes faster
  • Therapists spend their time where it is needed most, which takes pressure off care

Session prices: US consumer cost guides. Faster progress is what we expect from more practice; the pilot has to show it.

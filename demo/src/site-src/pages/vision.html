Rehab today. Strength for everyone tomorrow.

Vita starts where the need is greatest: rehabilitation, including the many older people who need it to stay independent. As the station gets cheaper and easier to find, the same arm can become the best way for anyone to train strength, because it knows the exact weight you can handle.

Where Vita is going

One machine, two steps.

  1. NowWe are here

    Rehabilitation and elderly care

    After a stroke, a shoulder problem or a new knee or hip, and for older people who need rehab to keep getting out of a chair, carrying shopping and climbing stairs. The specialist sets the plan; the arm guides and measures every repetition.

    Gyms, pools, care homes and community centres
  2. Later

    Strength training for everyone

    When stations become more affordable and more common, anyone can train with a coach that measures every repetition and sets the right weight for them.

    Every gym

Why it would be the best way to train

A dumbbell weighs the same every rep. You don't.

With a fixed weight, the first repetitions are too easy and the last ones are too hard. The arm measures how hard you push on every repetition and sets the resistance for the next one, so each rep lands in the zone where muscles grow.

How it optimises growth

Measure, adjust, grow.

Measure

Force, speed and range on every repetition. The arm feels when you slow down long before you notice it yourself.

Adjust

The resistance for the next repetition is set to what you can handle right now: not too light, not too heavy, always within safe limits.

Grow

Each session starts from your last measurements, so the load rises exactly as fast as you get stronger. No guessing, no plateaus from the wrong weight.

This is our long-term idea, not a product today. Vita is in development as a rehabilitation device. Training for everyone needs a lower price, more research and its own safety checks first.

Good to know

Questions families ask.

Does Vita replace carers or family?

No. Vita takes over the small questions and the reminders, and nudges towards people: call Sophie, go to the coffee morning. Care stays human.

My mother has dementia. Will she manage?

There is nothing to operate. Vita starts the conversation herself, speaks slowly and repeats things gladly, as often as needed.

What happens after a fall?

When Vita hears a loud sound that could be a fall, she asks twice within ten seconds whether everything is alright. An answer is enough to stop there. Without any reaction she calls the emergency contact and says which room it happened in. If the emergency contact does not pick up, Vita alerts the emergency services (112).

Where does what Vita hears go?

Anna’s voice is turned into text on the speaker, so the sound never leaves the house. What Vita remembers about her is kept in the home. To form a reply, the words are sent to our own servers in the EU, which store nothing and share nothing with anyone.

Does the fall alarm work without internet?

Hearing the fall and asking twice happen on the speaker itself, so they do not depend on our servers.

Is Vita a medical device?

No. Vita is a companion and a safety net at home. She does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor.

Insurance and reimbursement

Covered by insurance? Not yet.

Today families pay for Vita themselves. In the long run we want her to be reimbursed, like a personal alarm can be. That is a goal, not a promise.

  1. NowPrivate subscription. No reimbursement.
  2. NextPilots with care organisations and municipalities, to measure what Vita changes: fewer emergency calls, more time safely at home.
  3. LaterWith that evidence, apply for reimbursement through the municipality and through health insurers.
Good to know

Questions about the bill.

Can I claim Vita from my health insurance today?

No. Vita is not on any insurer’s list. Some supplementary policies reimburse a personal alarm when there is a medical indication; we want Vita to qualify in the same way, but she does not yet.

Will my price drop if insurance starts to pay?

If Vita becomes reimbursed, the covered part comes off your bill from that moment. We cannot pay back earlier months.

What does the €149 cover?

Someone comes to the house, places the speakers, connects them and introduces Vita to your parent and to the family.

Do we need internet at home?

Yes, Vita needs a connection. If there is none, we can add one for a small monthly extra.

What if it does not suit my mother?

Stop any month. We collect the speakers and the subscription ends.
